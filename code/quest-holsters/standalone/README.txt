GTAVXR Holsters and Native VR Weapon Wheel — community beta 1

1. Install GTAVXR 1.0.5 by LVL for GTA V Legacy Story Mode. Close the game.
2. Extract this entire download. Run “Set up holsters.cmd” and choose GTA5.exe in your Legacy installation. First-time setup uses the internet to download three checksum-verified NuGet packages. It reads the HUD from your game and generates the weapon pictures locally. No administrator access is needed.
3. In the Suite, open Mods > Add mod and choose QuestFourSlots.zip inside the extracted download. On Play, select a Story Mode profile, open Mods and enable QuestFourSlots. Use only one version of the holster script in that profile. Then press Play.

Tap grip at either hip or shoulder to draw with that hand; tap again to stow. Hold shoulder grip for the VR wheel, click the stick over a category to cycle, and release to equip. Release over empty space to cancel. To assign a weapon, tilt the right stick downward and hold while reaching into the slot. Two pulses confirm the save. Hold the right stick down at the wheel center to calibrate. Drag a visible orb with grip, then hold the right stick down to save and exit.

Set the holsters to fit your reach

1. Stand or sit as you normally play. Reach toward either shoulder until you feel the short vibration. Hold grip to open the VR wheel and keep holding it.
2. Move that same hand to the empty center of the wheel. With your right thumb, tilt the stick downward and hold for about a second. Keep the stick tilted instead of clicking it inward. When four orbs appear, release grip and let the stick spring back to center.
3. Bring either hand to an orb, squeeze and hold grip, move your hand to a comfortable draw position, then release grip to place it. Repeat for each hip and shoulder.
4. With grip released, tilt the right stick downward again and hold for about a second to save and exit. The orbs disappear. Reach back to each spot and feel for the short vibration to check the fit. You can recalibrate later the same way.

Works with the verified GTAVXR 1.0.5 executable. The development setup used Quest 3, Virtual Desktop and GTA V Legacy via Rockstar Games Launcher. The game files are read only. The separate beta keeps its layer, locally generated artwork, settings and diagnostic log under %LOCALAPPDATA%\GTAVXR-QuestHolsters-Beta. To stop using it, disable QuestFourSlots in your profile.

Known reports: sprinting while dual wielding is limited; the original weapon wheel can sometimes return after buying guns or attachments. To report an issue, include your headset, runtime, GTAVXR version and the steps leading to it.
